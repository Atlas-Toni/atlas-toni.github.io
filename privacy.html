Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what the app ATLAS Twizthub does with data. ATLAS Twizthub is a private tool run by the owner of the Twizthub channel (@twizthub). It connects only to Twizthub's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and the Twizthub Facebook Page.

What data the app uses

With the account owner's permission, the app reads:

  • basic account information of the @twizthub accounts (name, ID, follower count);
  • the list of videos posted by @twizthub;
  • statistics of those videos, such as views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, average watch time and new subscribers or followers;
  • public comments that people leave on @twizthub posts on Instagram and on the Twizthub Facebook Page (see Comments below).

The app can also publish videos to the @twizthub accounts, but only after the owner approves each video. The app does not read private messages. Apart from public comments, it does not collect data about viewers as individuals; for statistics it only uses totals that the platforms report.

Comments

To help the owner reply to comments, the app reads public comments on @twizthub posts on Instagram and on the Twizthub Facebook Page: the comment text, the public username or name of the person who wrote it, and the time it was posted.

The comment text is sent to an AI language service (Groq) to draft a suggested reply. The owner reads every suggestion, can change it, and decides whether to post it. Nothing is ever posted without the owner's approval. Replies are posted publicly as @twizthub, under the original comment.

When the owner changes a suggested reply, the app keeps the change as an example to write better suggestions. These examples are stored without names or usernames.

Comments are stored on the owner's own computer only for as long as needed to handle them. If you wrote a comment and want it removed from the app's records, email the address under Contact.

How the data is used

The data is used for two purposes: to measure how Twizthub's own videos perform, so the creator can make better videos, and to help the owner reply to comments on those videos. It is not used for advertising and it is never sold or rented. It is not shared with anyone, except that comment text is processed by Groq to draft replies, as described under Comments.

Where the data is stored

Statistics and comments are stored on the owner's own computer. Login tokens are stored in protected files on that computer and are never published. The owner can delete all stored data at any time.

Google and YouTube

ATLAS Twizthub uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies. ATLAS Twizthub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google via myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Instagram via the app's Settings → Apps and websites, for the Facebook Page via Settings → Business integrations, and for TikTok via Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. After access is removed, stored statistics and comments are deleted within 30 days or earlier on request.
